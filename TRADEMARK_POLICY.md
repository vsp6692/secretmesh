# SecretMesh Trademark Policy (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT: pending qualified legal review and owner approval.** This policy describes how the project intends to protect its name and logo. It does not claim a registered trademark. As of this draft, no trademark registration or clearance search is known to exist; see the open items at the end.

The [source-available licence](LICENSE.md) covers the Software's code. It **does not grant any right to use SecretMesh's names, logos or other brand elements** (section 6.3 of the licence). This policy says what is fine, what needs permission and why.

## 1. The marks

- **"SecretMesh"** (word mark) and the SecretMesh logo and visual identity (together, the "Marks"), owned by or claimed for [LICENSOR_LEGAL_NAME].
- Rights in the Marks arise from use and may be registered later. Use "SecretMesh™" until a registration exists; do not use ® yet.

## 2. What you can do without asking

- Use the name **to refer to the project accurately** (nominative use): "We run SecretMesh", "a SecretMesh connector", "compatible with SecretMesh", "SecretMesh integration for Terraform".
- Link to the project and quote its documentation with attribution.
- Name an **independent integration** descriptively, for example "Acme connector for SecretMesh", as long as it is clear that it is made by you.
- Write, teach, talk and blog about SecretMesh, and show screenshots of the unmodified software.
- Use the Marks as they appear in your own internal deployment (internal customisation of titles, colours and names for your own staff is fine).
- Run a public **unofficial fork** to prepare contributions, if you say plainly that it is an unofficial fork and do not use the Marks as the fork's product name.

## 3. What needs written permission

- Using "SecretMesh", or a confusingly similar name, as the **name of a product, company, domain, package, image or service** that you offer to others.
- A hosted, managed, resold, bundled, embedded or rebranded offering, including any that has a commercial licence. A commercial licence ([COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md)) states whether and how the Marks may be used, and white-label licences are priced separately.
- Using the logo on merchandise, in marketing that implies sponsorship or endorsement, or in app-store, marketplace or container-registry listings.
- Publishing a **modified version** under the SecretMesh name.

## 4. What is not allowed

- Implying that you, your product or your fork is the official SecretMesh, or is endorsed, certified or sponsored by the project, when it is not.
- Using the Marks in a way that is deceptive, disparaging or likely to confuse users about the source of the software.
- Registering the Marks, similar marks or lookalike domains and handles.
- Using the Marks to sell support, training or certification that suggests official status.

## 5. Forks and modified versions

A public Modified version must:

1. remove or replace the SecretMesh name and logo as its product identity;
2. say that it is derived from SecretMesh and is not official, in a place users see (README and UI footer);
3. keep the licence and copyright notices.

The licence's section 3 conditions apply. Internal forks used only within an organisation need not rename.

## 6. Contributor connectors and plugins

Connectors and plugins merged into the SecretMesh repository are part of SecretMesh and use its name. Third-party ones should use the descriptive form in section 2 ("Acme connector for SecretMesh").

## 8. Open items for the owner and counsel

1. **Clearance.** No search has been done for conflicting marks named "SecretMesh" or similar in the target classes (software, SaaS) and jurisdictions. A search is recommended before the commercial programme is announced.
2. **Owner.** The Marks must be owned by (or be assigned to) the same legal entity that is the Licensor. [LICENSOR_LEGAL_NAME] is a placeholder.
3. **Registration.** Decide whether to register, in which jurisdictions and classes, and whether the logo is registered separately.
4. **Domain and package names.** Secure matching domain, GitHub organisation, package and image names.
5. **Enforcement.** Trademark rights can weaken if not policed; decide who monitors and enforces.
